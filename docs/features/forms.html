Signup forms

A signup form collects email addresses, checks each one with a code, and puts every person into your audience. Turn on gamification and it becomes a waitlist, with a queue position, a referral link and a public leaderboard.

What it is

Each form is a document in Waitlists. Its members live under it, one document per address. A form can be used in two ways:

  • Its own page at /waitlist/{form}, which Arc CMS renders from the form's settings.
  • Your own markup on any page of your site: a plain HTML <form> marked with data-waitlist-form, which Arc CMS takes over when the page loads.

Every sign-up becomes a contact that joins the form's own list and any other lists the form feeds. From there, broadcasts and sequences reach them (Email marketing).

AddressWhat it shows
/waitlist/{form}The form's own signup page (the form is found by id or slug)
/leaderboard/{form}The public leaderboard: top 50 confirmed members, addresses masked
/leaderboard/{form}/{member}The leaderboard for one member, from the link in their welcome email

Turn it on or off

The feature id is forms. It is on by default and needs audience. To turn it off, add it to off in src/custom/features.ts:

export const CUSTOM_FEATURES: FeatureChoice = {
    off: ['forms'],
};

When it is off:

  • The Signup Forms menu item and every form page (/waitlist, /leaderboard, /user/{form}/{member}, /admin/waitlists) are gone and show the not-found page.
  • Form markup on your pages stays as you wrote it, but nothing reads, counts or submits it.
  • The functions in functions/src/features/forms.ts are not built, so the next deploy of all functions removes them. Leaderboard and member links in emails already sent stop working.
  • The forms, members and templates stay in Firestore. Turn the feature back on to have them again.

See Feature ids and Choose your app's features.

Set it up

  1. Set up email in Settings, Email Settings (see Email engine). Without a working provider no code can be sent, so every form confirms people without one.

  2. Open Signup Forms and click the add button. Give it a name. The slug is filled in from the name and becomes the form's id, the {form} in every address above. Editing the slug later changes the slug only, never the id, and breaks links that used the old slug.

  3. Creating the form also creates its list under Audience, Lists and its two email templates. Open the form's Email Templates to adjust them.

  4. Link to /waitlist/{form}, or put the form on a page of your site (next section).

Put a form on a page

Every page of your site binds every form that has data-waitlist-form: the home page (src/custom/site/home.html and its translations, see Build your home page), content pages and list pages from your templates, and static pages. It works in the app and on the published page, where public/assets/js/arc-site.js runs the same steps through the same functions:

<form data-waitlist-form data-waitlist-id="beta">
  <input name="firstName" placeholder="Your name">
  <input type="email" name="email" required>
  <input name="company" placeholder="Company">
  <button type="submit">Join the beta</button>
</form>
<p><span data-waitlist-count="beta"></span> people have joined</p>

If no form has the given id, the first visit creates one with default settings. The code step, the success panel with the referral link, and the error messages are drawn inside the form.

Put the form's id in data-waitlist-id, not a slug you changed later. The page finds the form by its slug, but sign-ups are sent with the value as written, and a value that is not the id fails with "This form does not exist".

Use it

What happens on a sign-up

  1. The visitor submits. The joinForm function finds or creates their member record on that form, with an 8-character referral code. Submitting the same address again returns the same record, never a second one.

  2. The member record creates a pending contact, which joins the form's list and the lists it feeds, gets the form's default tag and its mapped answers.

  3. requestFormOtp emails a 6-digit code. It is valid for 15 minutes, can be resent once a minute, and allows 5 wrong tries.

  4. The visitor enters it. verifyFormOtp checks it and finalizeFormSignup confirms the member and gives them the next queue position.

  5. The contact becomes subscribed, the waitlist.joined event fires (see Automations), and the welcome email goes out.

  6. If the visitor arrived through a referral link, creditReferral records the referral and the referrer's count goes up by one.

The code step is skipped, and the member confirmed at once, when email is not set up, when the Waitlist emails switch in Settings, Email Settings is off, or when the form's verification template is inactive. Such a member is confirmed but not verified.

Emails

Each form has its own two templates, on its Email Templates page (/admin/waitlists/templates/{form}):

TemplateKindSent
Verification code (waitlist_verify_otp_email)TransactionalOn every sign-up that needs a code. Uses ##OTP##
Welcome (waitlist_welcome_email)MarketingWhen the member verifies their address. Uses ##WAITLIST##, ##REFERRAL_LINK##, ##LEADERBOARD_LINK##

A form's welcome can instead be the first step of a sequence on its list: the migrateWelcomeToSequences admin callable moves every form's welcome there and marks the form, after which the sequence sends it and the template is not sent directly (see Email marketing).

To turn a form's code step off, make its verification template inactive. For more emails after the welcome, add a sequence on the form's list (Email marketing). Every tag is listed in Email merge tags.

Deleting the verification template does not turn the code step off: the default template is created again the next time a code is needed. Switch it to inactive instead.

The welcome template goes out when an address is verified. A form that confirms people without a code never sends it. For a welcome on such a form, use a sequence on the form's list with a first step at 0 hours.

Referrals and the leaderboard

Each member gets a referral link, the site's home page with ?ref= and their code. A visitor who opens it keeps the code in their browser for 24 hours and passes it on when they sign up. Codes belong to one form: signing up on another form credits nobody. Referring yourself, or the same address twice, is not counted.

The leaderboard ranks confirmed members by referrals, most first, then by who signed up earliest. It shows first names and masked addresses only.

Members

View Users on a form lists its members, with notes and the form's own tags. Each form's dashboard shows sign-up numbers, traffic sources, devices, top referrers and recent sign-ups, and exports the members.

  • Deleting a member takes one off their referrer's count and takes the contact off the form's own list. The contact itself stays, and so do the other lists the form fed.
  • Deleting a form deletes all its members, their notes and referrals, takes every contact off the form's list and deletes that list.
  • To remove a person everywhere, erase the contact (see Audience): that deletes their member records too.

Extend it

Any page that renders your own HTML can collect sign-ups by linking to /waitlist/{form}. To react to a sign-up in code or without code, listen for the waitlist.joined event with an automation rule (Automations), or add a sequence on the form's list.

The public functions are callable from any client, but prefer the built-in page and markup: they already call joinForm, requestFormOtp, verifyFormOtp, finalizeFormSignup and creditReferral in the right order. Calling them from a client uses the prefixed name, such as arccms-joinForm.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause and fix
"Please wait 42s before requesting another code"The one-code-a-minute limit per address and form. Wait and resend.
No code arrivesOpen Email Logs: a skipped log names the reason, such as email_disabled or feature_disabled. See Email engine.
"This form has no verification email template, so signups cannot be confirmed"Email is on but the form's code template could not be found or made. Open the form's Email Templates page to restore the defaults.
"This email has not been verified"The form needs a code and none was verified for that address. Request a new code.
A form on the site does nothingIt lacks data-waitlist-form, the published page was built before the form was added (publish it again), the form is not Active, or forms is off.
A referral was not countedThe code belongs to another form, it is a self-referral or repeat, or more than 24 hours passed between the click and the sign-up in that browser.
"Save form answers as" is missing from the form's settingsIt lists the input names held in the form document's fields list, and nothing fills that list for you. The mapping itself is the form's fieldMap (input name to contact field key), which an admin can also write directly.
