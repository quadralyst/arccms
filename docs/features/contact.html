Contact form

A form on any page of your site that people use to write to you. Their messages land in Messages in the admin, and admins get an alert.

What it is

A plain HTML form marked data-arc-contact-form, on any published page. The site's script, arc-site.js, sends it to the submitContactMessage function, which checks it for spam and stores it. Admins read the messages under Messages and reply from their own email client. Arc CMS sends nothing to the person who wrote, and does not add them to your audience.

Turn it on or off

The contact form is a switchable feature, id contact. It is on by default and needs no other feature. To turn it off, list it in src/custom/features.ts:

export const CUSTOM_FEATURES: FeatureChoice = {
    off: ['contact'],
};

When it is off, the Messages menu item and page are gone, contact forms are removed from published pages, and its two functions are not deployed. Messages already received stay in Firestore. See Feature ids.

Set it up

  1. Deploy the functions, the Firestore rules and the indexes.
  2. Add a form to a page of your site: 
    <form data-arc-contact-form>
    <input name="name" placeholder="Your name">
    <input name="email" type="email" required placeholder="Your email">
    <textarea name="message" required placeholder="How can we help?"></textarea>
    <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>
  3. Deploy the website.

Use it

The form may have inputs named name, email, phone, subject and message; email and message are required, and other inputs are ignored. Publishing adds a line under the form ("We use your details only to reply.") with a link to your privacy page when you have one. After sending, the form is replaced by a thank-you.

Under Messages, messages are in three tabs: New, Done and Possible spam. Reply opens your email client with the message quoted. Mark done, Not spam and Delete do what they say.

Extend it

Style the form with your site's CSS; the thank-you is .arc-contact-sent, an error .arc-contact-error and the privacy line .arc-contact-notice. Its text can be translated through strings/{lang}.json with the contact_* keys.

What it stores

One document per message in ContactMessages: name, email, phone, subject, message, page, lang, status (new, done or spam) and createdAt. Messages are kept until an admin deletes them. See Data model.

Who can do what

Anyone can send a message through the function; nobody can write ContactMessages from a browser. Admins read messages, change their status and delete them.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause and fix
The form does nothingThe page has no arc-site.js (deploy the website), or submitContactMessage is not deployed.
"You have sent several messages already"Five messages an hour from one address or one email. It resets within the hour.
A message is not under NewLook under Possible spam: a message with more than three links goes there and raises no alert.
