Merchant payments

Each customer of an app, such as a shop or a clinic, takes money from their own buyers on their own provider account. The money never passes through the site's owner.

What it is

Server helpers in the app kit, a connector for each provider behind one interface, and the webhook, sweep and checks that hold the rules: no browser holds a key, a forged event changes nothing, and only the provider's own answer can say paid. It has no screen of its own: the app's screens use it, in the app's languages. Payments is the other need, selling the site's own plans through one account.

Turn it on or off

Off unless an app turns it on, with on: ['merchant-payments'] in src/custom/features.ts (Choose your app's features). While off, its four functions are not deployed and its helpers answer off.

Set it up

Deploy the functions and the rules, and fill in functions/src/custom/payments.ts. A merchant connects with their provider's keys through the app's own screen, and adds the webhook address in the provider's admin. Worldline Connect and Payrexx cannot be picked until each has been proven on the provider's own account: see Prove a connector.

Use it

From an app's own functions: startPayment() gives the page to send the buyer to, the result reaches the app's onResult, and readPayment(), endPayment() and refundPayment() do the rest. The whole of it is in Take payments on a merchant's account.

Extend it

A new provider is one file and one line, and changes no app: Add a provider. Build and test against the pretend provider.

What it stores

Accounts, keys, webhook addresses, payments and events, all in collections named Merchant...: see the Data model. Keys are stored in Firestore where no browser can read them, as AiKeys are, so a full export of the database by someone with server access includes them. No buyer's details are kept, and no raw event.

Who can do what

The app decides who may connect, by wrapping each helper in its own function. A browser reads only what the app's rules allow of an account's public view and of a payment; admins can read both. Nobody writes them from a browser, and nobody reads a key from one.
