The app kit

Your own Cloud Functions in functions/src/custom/ use Arc CMS's accounts, claims, sign-in and email through one import, functions/src/app-kit/index.ts. What it exports is supported: it keeps its meaning across Arc CMS updates, and a test fails if it changes without this page.

import { createAppAccount, issueSignInToken, mergeAppClaims } from '../app-kit/index.js';

Everything else in functions/src is core's to change. You may read it to learn how something works, but import from the kit, and ask for a missing export in Arc CMS rather than reaching past it.

Exports

ExportWhat it doesGuide
createAppAccount()Creates a sign-in account and its users record for a person with no email and no phone. Takes { displayName, claims?, uid?, selfService? }, returns { uid, userDocId }. The account is locked unless selfService is true.App accounts
deleteAppAccount()Deletes a person's account by their Auth uid; Arc CMS's account deletion does the rest and announces user.deleted.App accounts
restoreAppSignIn()Gives an app account its sign-in back after the browser deleted it: the same uid, the record's name, Arc CMS's claims and the ones you pass, in one write. Safe to repeat.App accounts
APP_ACCOUNT'app': the by value of a record createAppAccount() made.App accounts
mergeAppClaims()Merges your own claims into any account: refuses arccms_ and Firebase's reserved names and anything over Firebase's 1000-byte limit, never replaces what is there, removes a claim with null.App accounts
isArcAdmin()Whether an ID token (request.auth?.token) or an account's claims make the holder an Arc CMS admin, for a callable only admins may use.App accounts
revokeSessions()Signs a person out everywhere: their sessions end at the next token refresh, within the hour.App accounts
issueSignInToken()A custom token for a uid, which the browser exchanges with signInWithCustomToken. When Google Cloud cannot sign tokens yet, it says so and alerts the admins.App accounts
signedInByApp()Whether a callable's caller signed in with your sign-in token (signedInByApp(request)), never with a sign-in linked in the browser. The callable twin of the rules helper of the same name.App accounts
createPinStore()A place for your app's PINs, apart from Arc CMS's phone PINs: createPinStore(namespace, { maxAttempts, strength }) gives set, check, has, clearLock and remove.PINs for your app
createPhonePinCheck()Checks the signed-in person's phone sign-in PIN at a gate inside your app, such as a parent area, counting wrong tries apart from sign-in: createPhonePinCheck(namespace, { maxAttempts }) gives has, check and clearLock. Never for signing in.PINs for your app
APP_PINS'app_pins': the collection every app PIN is kept in, closed to the browser.PINs for your app
isValidPin()Whether a value is a PIN at all: exactly 6 digits.PINs for your app
isWeakPin()Whether a 6-digit PIN is too easy to guess (one digit repeated, a straight run, a common pattern).PINs for your app
pinTooEasy()Whether a new PIN is refused under the app's strength: isWeakPin() when it is strict, never when it is simple. pinTooEasy(pin, strength?); leave the strength out for the app's.PINs for your app
signInStrength()The password and PIN strength the app chose in src/custom/sign-in.ts: 'strict' or 'simple'.Set up sign-in
consumeRateLimit()Counts one use of a key in a time window and refuses with resource-exhausted once the limit is reached: consumeRateLimit(key, max, windowMs, message, reason?). The refusal's details carry reason (default too-many-attempts) and retryAfter, the seconds until the window reopens.PINs for your app
hashedKey()A rate-limit key you choose, such as a device id, hashed and kept apart from Arc CMS's keys: hashedKey('kiosk-device', deviceId).PINs for your app
callerKey()A rate-limit key for the caller's address, hashed. { trustedProxies: 1 } for a function reached through a Hosting rewrite.PINs for your app
sendAppEmail()Sends a transactional email from your functions: one of your templates (functions/src/custom/email-templates.ts) in a language, or a subject and body you built, inside the site's email layout, to 1 to 50 addresses, with files. Returns one log per address; the outcome comes back as AppEvents.Send email from your app
captureAppEmails()For your unit tests: records what sendAppEmail would send (recipients, template, language, data, attachments) without Firestore, Storage or a provider, until stop().Send email from your app
escapeEmailHtml()Text made safe to put inside HTML you pass as { html } tag data, such as the cells of a table.Send email from your app
MAX_APP_EMAIL_RECIPIENTS50: the most addresses one sendAppEmail call takes.Send email from your app
MAX_ATTACHMENTS10: the most files on one email.Send email from your app
MAX_ATTACHMENT_BYTES5 MB: the most bytes all of one email's files may come to.Send email from your app
APP_EMAIL_EVENTSThe AppEvents types an app email's outcome is written as: email.sent, email.failed, email.bounced.Send email from your app

Errors a person can act on are HttpsErrors with code invalid-argument or not-found, so a callable can let them through as they are.
