Launch checklist

Go through this list on the production project, in order, before you point your domain at the site. Every item is something Arc CMS provides; the second column says where to check it.

Accounts

CheckWhere
[ ] Only the people who run the site have the admin role. There are two roles, admin and user, and an admin can do everything.Users; see Users and roles
[ ] Visitors can create accounts only if your site needs them. Signing up to a form needs no account.Settings, User Settings, Enable User Signups

Email

CheckWhere
[ ] A real provider is active (not the Debug Provider, which only logs), the connection test passed, and the sender name and address are yours.Settings, Email Settings
[ ] Logo, colors, footer text and physical address are set for Composer and broadcast emails, and each signup form's two emails carry your logo and address too.Brand Kit, under Email + SMS (or Email); each form's Email Templates
[ ] A test email arrives in an inbox, not in spam.Settings, Email Settings, Send a Test Email; then Email Logs
[ ] The unsubscribe and preferences links in a list email open a working page, and unsubscribing stops further marketing email.Send a broadcast to a list with only yourself on it; see Email engine

Sign-ups

CheckWhere
[ ] Signing up on the live home page with your own address sends the code and the welcome email, and you then appear in the form's Users and in Contacts.Your home page; Collect sign-ups
[ ] The terms notice on every signup form links to your terms and privacy policy. It links only pages you publish, so until then the words show as plain text.The Terms and Privacy Policy standard pages, published; Write static pages
[ ] The home page, its title, the header and the footer show your brand and links, not Arc CMS's "not set up yet" placeholder.src/custom/site/home.html, src/custom/site/header.html, src/custom/site/footer.html; Build your home page

Legal pages and banners

CheckWhere
[ ] The Privacy Policy, Terms and Cookie Policy are written for your site, checked for where you work, and published.Content, Info Pages; The standard pages
[ ] No published page still holds a [Replace: prompt. Open each standard page you published and search it for [Replace:.Content, Info Pages
[ ] The sign-in page shows your name, logo and colours, not Arc CMS's.Settings, About; src/custom/site/sign-in.html, src/custom/site/site.css; Brand the sign-in page
[ ] About and Contact are filled in and published, and the footer lists them.Content, Info Pages; The standard pages
[ ] Settings, About has the contact email, public phone, address and social links you want shown; the Contact page and your footer print them.Settings, About; Let editors change your home page
[ ] Sending the contact form on the live Contact page puts the message in Messages and alerts you./info/contact; Messages; Contact form
[ ] The cookie banner has your wording and links to your cookie policy (the published Cookie Policy page, unless you set a link). It shows only on pages the app draws (sign-in, form pages, search, the member area), not on published pages.Settings, Site Usage
[ ] You have chosen whether to show "Powered by Arc CMS" on pages and emails.Settings, Miscellaneous, Branding

Search engines and AI

CheckWhere
[ ] Site Name, Production URL (your real domain) and the publisher identity are filled in.Settings, About; Get found by search engines and AI
[ ] Crawler choices are saved and applied.Settings, Discoverability, Save and apply to site
[ ] /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml open on the live site and list addresses on your domain.Your browser
[ ] Each published page has a meta description, and the main pages score 80 or more in the Checks tab.The editor's SEO and Checks tabs
[ ] The site is added to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, with the sitemap submitted.Links on the Discoverability screen
[ ] The search index is complete and a search on the live site finds your pages.Settings, Search, Rebuild everything

Analytics

CheckWhere
[ ] Your Firebase web configuration has your own Google Analytics measurement id, so visits to the pages the app draws (sign-in, form pages, search, the member area) are counted. Published pages (the home page, content pages and static pages) carry no analytics tag, so their visits are not counted.Analytics; Configure a project
[ ] Google Analytics is connected, so the admin dashboard shows traffic.Settings, Analytics; Analytics

Deploy and domain

CheckWhere
[ ] Your feature choice is final before the full deploy.src/custom/features.ts; Choose your website's features
[ ] Everything is deployed to the production project: functions, database rules and indexes, storage rules, and the website. The guided deploy then publishes every page again by itself.npm run deploy, then Everything; Deploy
[ ] The security rules on the project are the ones in the repository.Security rules
[ ] Your domain is connected to the project's Firebase Hosting site. Arc CMS has no command for this: add the domain in the Firebase console, under Hosting.Firebase console
[ ] After the domain works, the Production URL is set to it and every page is published again, so every page, the sitemap and the feeds use the domain.Settings, About; then Content, Content types, Republish website (or npm run deploy, then Website)

After launch

You are done with this path. For day-to-day work in the admin area, see Use the admin area; when something goes wrong, see Troubleshooting.
