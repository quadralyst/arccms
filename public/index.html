Automatic Waitlists

Capture, verify, and manage email signups with a beautiful dashboard. Export your subscriber list anytime. No coding required—just drop in your HTML and Arc CMS handles the rest.

Email Verification

Ensure quality signups with built-in email verification. Automatically send confirmation emails and prevent fake or spam addresses from cluttering your waitlist.

Email Automation

Set up welcome emails, drip sequences, and broadcasts without any coding. Use pre-built templates or customize your own. Track opens and clicks to measure engagement.

Viral Referral System

Turn every signup into a growth engine. Each subscriber gets a unique referral link automatically. Track who referred whom and reward your top referrers.

Global Leaderboard
