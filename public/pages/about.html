About GoWow

We build the infrastructure that lets goodness find its way — connecting volunteers, corporates, donors and NGOs on one verified platform, so that giving is measurable rather than hopeful.

Last updated 14 March 2025 6 min read

Our mission

Make it as easy to give time or money well as it is to order something online — and just as easy to see what happened next.

Verified by default

Every organisation on the platform is checked before it can post anything. No exceptions, and no paid shortcuts.

Outcomes, not intentions

Hours are confirmed by the organisation that received them. A number on this site means the work actually happened.

Built in India

Designed around how giving actually works here — small NGOs, local drives, and a lot of people who want to help nearby.

Why we exist

Most people want to help, and most organisations need help. The two rarely find each other, and when they do there is usually no record that anything happened. That gap — not a shortage of goodwill — is the problem GoWow was built to close.

We are not a charity and we do not hold donations. We are the layer underneath: verification, matching, logging and reporting, so that everyone else can get on with the actual work.

How we started

GoWow began in 2022 with a spreadsheet and eleven volunteers in Nagpur, trying to staff a weekend teaching drive. The spreadsheet did not survive the second week. What replaced it eventually became the platform.

The first version did one thing: it told an NGO who was actually going to turn up. That turned out to be the whole product.

From the founding team's first note to partners

What we believe

  • Verification is not optional. An unverified listing costs somebody a wasted Saturday, and costs the sector its credibility.
  • The record belongs to the person who earned it. Your hours are yours, portable, and provable.
  • Small and regular beats large and occasional. Our scoring, our programmes and our reporting are all built around that.
  • If we cannot measure it, we do not claim it.

How we are funded

GoWow is free for volunteers and for NGOs, and always will be. The platform is funded by corporate CSR partnerships, programme sponsorships and a small margin on the eMarketplace. We do not take a cut of donations.

Who we work with

Verified NGOs, corporate CSR teams, colleges running community credit programmes, and city partners who host our programmes locally. Every partnership is listed publicly, including the ones that ended.

Where we are going

Two things over the next year: deeper verification, so that a GoWow record is accepted without question by an employer or a university; and wider coverage, so that a listing near you is a realistic expectation rather than a lucky break.

If you would like to bring GoWow to a city we are not in yet, the fastest route is the partnership form.
