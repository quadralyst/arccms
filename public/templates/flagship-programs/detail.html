What the KPL actually is

Most impact programmes ask individuals to give a little, quietly, on their own. The Karma Premier League does the opposite: it makes giving a team sport, puts it on a public leaderboard, and runs it hard for thirty days.

Teams register in their own city. Each team picks a cause track, then spends the season completing actions posted by verified NGOs — teaching sessions, collection drives, clean-ups, skill workshops. Every completed action is confirmed by the host organisation before it scores, so the table reflects work that genuinely happened.

"Our office team joined for the bragging rights. By week three nobody was talking about the leaderboard any more — they were talking about the school."

Rohit N., team captain, Pune

The four cause tracks

Every season runs four tracks. A team commits to one at registration and stays with it for the full thirty days, which keeps the work deep rather than scattered.

  • Learn — teaching, mentoring, exam prep and digital literacy at partner schools.
  • Green — plantation, clean-ups, waste segregation and urban gardening.
  • Care — ration and supply distribution, elder care, shelter support.
  • Build — repairs, painting, infrastructure and equipment drives at community sites.

How scoring works

Points are weighted so that sustained effort beats one big day. A team that shows up every week will finish ahead of a team that runs a single large event, by design.

Season scoring weightings are published in full at the start of each edition.

Who runs it

The league is coordinated by GoWow with a city partner in each location — usually a college, a corporate CSR team or an established NGO. City partners verify local teams, host the opening briefing and run the closing ceremony.

If you would like to bring the KPL to a city that is not yet on the list, get in touch through the programme page and we will send you the city partner pack.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to enter?

No. Registration is free for every team. Some actions involve materials or travel, and those are always stated on the listing before you commit.

Can a team be spread across cities?

Yes, but the team is ranked in the city where its captain registered. Remote actions count towards the team total.

What if we cannot complete an action we signed up for?

Release it from the app at least 24 hours ahead and it returns to the pool with no penalty. Late drops cost the team points, because an NGO planned around you.

Do individual members get credit too?

Every verified hour also lands in that member's own record — Wow Coins and Social Civil Score points, exactly as if they had volunteered outside the league.
